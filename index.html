About and FAQ

The project stays focused on useful learning.

Why lokii.tech?

lokii.tech exists to make CS and cybersecurity learning clear, structured, and practical. It guides learners from beginner-friendly foundations to real tools, workflows, projects, and professional thinking, helping them build confidence and turn learning into real evidence of skill.

The site is open to useful corrections, clearer notes, extra resources, and meaningful contributions in the direction of the objective.

What is the goal of the project?

The goal is to make quality education freely accessible, structured, practical, and meaningful. The project follows the FOSS spirit: share useful work, let people inspect and improve it, and keep a serious learning path open for the next learner.

Who should use this site?

Students, self-taught learners, and early security practitioners should use this site if they want practical computer science and cybersecurity notes without getting buried in random tutorials or paid course funnels.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with the Intro Level resources. Cybersecurity basics, operating systems, networking, access control, and shell scripting are ordered to build the foundation before tools and projects.

How are the notes designed?

The notes are designed to turn learning into practice. Each guide explains the concept, why it matters, how it applies to real technical work, and what to do next. Many notes also include practice questions, practical tool guides, cheat sheets, examples, and after-note activities to support clear, structured, and meaningful learning.

What makes the project material useful?

The Pro Level material focuses on portfolio-ready work: scope, time estimates, deliverables, analysis discipline, reporting, and lessons learned. It helps people stuck in tutorial loops build meaningful projects in a clear stepwise way.

What other things can I check out?

You can also explore my other community-focused projects, including Fisholophy for writings and reflections, Fumic for offline media playback, Frodigy for productivity and tracking, and my professional profiles at saksham.info.np and sakshamsharma.com.np.

How can I contribute?

Contribute through the lokii.tech GitHub repository. Open an issue for corrections or suggestions, or send a pull request when you want to improve notes, links, structure, examples, or clarity.
