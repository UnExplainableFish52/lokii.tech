About and FAQ

The project stays focused on useful learning.

Why does lokii.tech exist?

lokii.tech is part of a simple free-education belief: quality learning should not be locked away from people who are curious, disciplined, and willing to work. Education becomes stronger when useful material is open, reusable, and improved in public.

The developer knows computer science, cybersecurity, Linux, networking, and related technical subjects best, so that is what this site focuses on. The larger message is broader than CS: good educational content should be accessible to everyone who needs it.

What does the FOSS mindset mean here?

The project follows the spirit of free and open-source software: share what you know, let others inspect it, improve it, and keep the path open for the next learner. It is not anti-quality; it is the opposite. Free education should still be serious, structured, and worth reading.

Who should use this site?

Students, self-taught learners, and early security practitioners who want practical computer science and cybersecurity notes without getting buried in random tutorials or paid course funnels.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with the Intro Level resources. Cybersecurity basics, operating systems, networking, access control, and shell scripting are ordered to build the foundation before tools and projects.

How are the notes designed?

Each guide should explain the concept, show why it matters, connect it to real technical work, and point toward the next useful step. The goal is practical understanding, not filler.

What makes the project material useful?

The Pro Level material focuses on portfolio-ready work: scope, time estimates, deliverables, analysis discipline, reporting, and lessons learned that can prove capability.

Where can I read more from the developer?

For broader writing, reflections, and educational content outside the focused CS notes on this site, read the developer's blog.

How can I contribute?

Open an issue or pull request on GitHub. Good contributions make the material clearer, more accurate, more current, or more useful for learners.
